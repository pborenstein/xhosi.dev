## Criticism From the Left
- Manzur criticized him for opposing Medicare for All and for his support of Israel. She says she is "aligned" with him but has not endorsed him ([Patch](https://new.patch.com/new-hampshire/portsmouth-nh/manzur-i-have-not-endorsed-pappas)).
- Manzur said the Laken Riley Act "empowered ICE to terrorize our communities" ([InDepthNH](https://indepthnh.org/2026/09/05/manzur-pappas-debate-over-ice-reform-support-for-israel-and-funding-police/)).
- He has received at least $200,000 from pro-Israel donors ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Pappas_(American_politician))).

## Criticism From the Right
- The Sununu campaign says he "catered to the Far Left" in the primary ([Patch](https://new.patch.com/new-hampshire/portsmouth-nh/manzur-i-have-not-endorsed-pappas)).
