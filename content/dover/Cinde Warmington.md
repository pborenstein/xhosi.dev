## Criticism From the Left
- Kathy Sullivan, a former chair of the New Hampshire Democratic Party, criticized Warmington’s past work for the opioid industry and said the dangers of opioids were known when Warmington was involved in that work. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinde_Warmington)
- Recovery advocates and first responders criticized Warmington’s $49,000 campaign contribution from Michael J. O’Connell and urged her to leave the 2024 governor’s race. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cinde_Warmington)

## Criticism From the Right
- The Ayotte campaign criticized Warmington’s past lobbying for Purdue Pharma and other opioid manufacturers, her defense of OxyContin, and her work for PMC Medical Group. [Laconia Daily Sun](https://www.laconiadailysun.com/news/state/ayotte-draws-first-major-democratic-challenger/article_f2ad48f7-bfaf-5b78-9e53-a7315c4bb57b.html)
- Ayotte campaign spokesman John Corbett said Warmington had made money from pharmaceutical companies that harmed Granite Staters and was disqualified from serving as governor. [Laconia Daily Sun](https://www.laconiadailysun.com/news/state/ayotte-draws-first-major-democratic-challenger/article_f2ad48f7-bfaf-5b78-9e53-a7315c4bb57b.html)
