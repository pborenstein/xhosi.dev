- Democratic primary opponent Maura Sullivan accused Shaheen of spreading claims that most of Sullivan’s campaign funding came from outside New Hampshire. Sullivan called the claims “lies”; the report does not state a response from Shaheen ([USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/09/08/new-hampshire-primary-2026-live-updates-results-ballot-election-candidates--live/91608075007/)).

- Shaheen’s opponents raised questions about her association with Dean Kamen through the Advanced Regenerative Manufacturing Institute.
