## Positions
| Issue | Stated position |
|---|---|
| Local government / probate office | Nedelka describes the Register of Probate office as “purely vestigial” after the 2011 restructuring of the probate court system. He supports amending the New Hampshire Constitution to eliminate the elected office and says professional county and probate-court staff could continue serving the public without it ([Strafford County Democratic Committee](https://straffordcountydemocraticcommittee.org/candidates/jan-nedelka/); [Dover Democrats](https://dovernhdemocrats.org/my-reps/jan-nedelka/)). |
| Families and marriage policy | Nedelka says that, if the office is reconstituted, he would advocate for all people and families in New Hampshire rather than only families described by a political party as “ordained by God.” He cites the 2026 GOP platform’s stated position favoring marriage between one man and one woman ([Dover Democrats](https://dovernhdemocrats.org/my-reps/jan-nedelka/)). |
