## Positions
| Issue | Stated position |
|---|---|
| Cost of living and taxes | Opposes broad-based income and sales taxes; supports phasing out the Interest and Dividends tax. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Health care | Supported private, opt-in family and medical leave in the 2020 survey; opposed a mandatory statewide family and medical leave program. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Abortion | Supported abortion restrictions after the first and second trimesters and opposed a guarantee of abortion before 24 weeks; stated that abortion should generally be limited to cases of rape, incest, or danger to the mother’s health. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| LGBTQ rights | Supported banning discussion of sexual orientation and gender identity in kindergarten through third grade. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Education and school funding | Supports Education Freedom Accounts, majority local-property-tax funding for public schools, and statewide standards-based assessments; opposes Common Core standards. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) [The Ballot Brief](https://theballotbrief.com/candidate/joseph-d-kenney-nh) |
| Housing | Opposed encouraging local zoning changes for housing development in 2022, citing local control; in 2018, stated that state government should do more to increase affordable-housing supply. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Immigration | Supported state and local law-enforcement cooperation with federal authorities on immigration enforcement. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Democracy and voting | Opposed mail-in voting, while supporting absentee ballots for health, military-service, or other reasons; supported strict residency requirements for voter registration. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Environment and energy | Supported New Hampshire’s Renewable Portfolio Standard and extending it; opposed the Northern Pass as proposed; said renewable-energy development should be cost-effective and locally controlled. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Marijuana | Opposes recreational-marijuana legalization and supports medical marijuana for chronically ill adults. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Guns | Opposed stricter gun laws beyond enforcing existing laws. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/joseph-kenney) |
| Foreign policy | No position was found in the supplied sources. |
