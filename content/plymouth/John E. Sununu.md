## Free State / MAGA Ties
- Donald Trump endorsed Sununu’s 2026 Senate campaign and described him as an “America First Patriot” who would advance the “America First Agenda” ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.sg/who-john-sununu-former-senator-wins-new-hampshire-gop-primary-93568)).
- Sununu supported Nikki Haley in the 2024 Republican presidential primary and described Trump as a “closer” in a January 2024 opinion piece ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.sg/who-john-sununu-former-senator-wins-new-hampshire-gop-primary-93568)).
- No Free State Project membership or affiliation is identified in the evidence ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/john-e-sununu); [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/elections/2026/09/08/new-hampshire-us-senate-primary-results-live-brown-pappas-sununu-run/)).
- The evidence does not explicitly describe Sununu as “MAGA,” although it reports Trump’s endorsement and America First characterization ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.sg/who-john-sununu-former-senator-wins-new-hampshire-gop-primary-93568)).

## Criticism From the Left
- Chris Pappas’s campaign accused Sununu of supporting pharmaceutical and insurance interests while in Congress and later working in industries connected to those sectors. Pappas’s campaign also cited a $35 million increase in Sununu’s net worth; the report says it did not independently verify the figure ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.sg/who-john-sununu-former-senator-wins-new-hampshire-gop-primary-93568)).
- The New Hampshire Democratic Party criticized Sununu’s opposition to the 2003 Climate Stewardship Act, saying he acted “against reducing greenhouse gases” ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_E._Sununu)).

## Criticism From the Right
- Republican primary opponent Scott Brown argued that Sununu was relying too heavily on the Sununu family name and its recognition in New Hampshire ([IBTimes](https://www.ibtimes.sg/who-john-sununu-former-senator-wins-new-hampshire-gop-primary-93568)).
