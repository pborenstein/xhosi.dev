## Positions
| Issue | Stated position |
|---|---|
| Cost of living and taxes | Opposes a broad-based income tax at present, but says it might be necessary if federal education aid ended; supports restoring reduced revenue streams and redirecting Education Freedom Account voucher money to public schools ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Health care | Supports funding the Office of the Child Advocate; voted for permanent Medicaid expansion in 2023 and for medical aid in dying in 2024 ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Abortion | Says abortion should be a private decision made by a woman in consultation with her doctor; supports a right to abortion before 24 weeks and repeal of the ban after 24 weeks ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| LGBTQ rights | Opposes allowing segregation based on biological sex and opposed bills restricting gender identity, transgender care, and sex-related content in schools ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Education and school funding | Supports increased public-school funding, opposes school-district tax-cap mandates, opposes Education Freedom Accounts, and opposes public-school open enrollment with limits ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Housing | Has expressed concern about state bills overriding local zoning; voted to keep the Housing Champion designation and grant program and opposed no-cause eviction proposals ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Immigration | Opposes requiring local immigration enforcement and voted to continue state involvement in refugee resettlement ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Democracy and voting | Said no voter should be turned away at the polls and that the affidavit policy was working well; opposed additional proof-of-ID requirements for some absentee voters ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Environment and energy | Supports renewable portfolio standards, voted for greenhouse-gas reduction goals, supported PFAS regulation, and has expressed caution about nuclear development ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
| Local school meals | Supported a motion to kill SB 204, concerning free student meals and reimbursement for meals provided at no cost ([NHPolitics](https://nhpolitics.org/representative/19745)). |
| Education Freedom Accounts | Opposes the Education Freedom Account program ([Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/sallie-fellows)). |
