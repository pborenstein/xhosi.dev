## Criticism From the Right
- Republicans criticized her use of campaign funds for personal car repairs, food, and travel, and accused her of corruption. New Hampshire Republican Party Chairman Jim MacEachern said, “It’s shameful that she is still in her position.” [NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2025-09-08/as-the-top-democrat-in-concord-liot-hill-is-a-rising-voice-and-a-growing-target)
- Republicans criticized her use of government email to help a national Democratic law firm find plaintiffs for a legal challenge to a Republican-backed voter-registration law. Deputy House Majority Leader Joe Sweeney called for her resignation or impeachment; Hill characterized the criticism as partisan politics. [NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2025-09-08/as-the-top-democrat-in-concord-liot-hill-is-a-rising-voice-and-a-growing-target)
- Joseph Sweeney launched an impeachment effort in August 2025, alleging that Hill improperly used her office to help a private law firm recruit clients. Hill said she was targeted because she is the sole Democrat on the Executive Council. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Liot_Hill)
