## Positions
| Issue | Stated position |
|---|---|
| Abortion | Supports guaranteeing the right to abortion before 24 weeks; opposes first- and second-trimester abortion bans; opposes repealing the ban after 24 weeks. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Education and school funding | Supports Education Freedom Accounts and having a majority of public-school funding come through local property taxes. On school-budget caps, he said decisions should be made locally rather than by state mandate and supported House Bill 1300’s approach of referring the decision to local voters. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Housing | Supports encouraging local zoning changes for housing development, while distinguishing encouragement from mandates. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Business taxes | Supports decreasing business taxes. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Income and property-related taxes | Opposes a broad-based income tax and reinstating the interest-and-dividends tax. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Energy and environment | Supports nuclear development. He opposed expanding net-metering opportunities, citing affordability and possible grid-upgrade costs. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Voting and democracy | Supports requiring new voters to provide proof of U.S. citizenship when registering to vote. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Sex-segregated facilities | Supports allowing segregation based on biological sex. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Marijuana | Undecided on legalization through home-growing and private use without sales; opposes licensed growers and private retail locations and state-run cannabis stores. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
| Child advocacy | Undecided on funding the Office of the Child Advocate. [Citizens Count](https://www.citizenscount.org/candidate/conrad-schoeffter) |
