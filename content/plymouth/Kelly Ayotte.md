## Positions
| Issue | Stated position |
|---|---|
| Cost of living | Vetoed legislation that would have doubled tolls for drivers without a New Hampshire E-ZPass, saying she opposed raising tolls and wanted to make New Hampshire more affordable and attractive to tourists ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/politics/2026-07-03/ayotte-vetoes-nine-bills-including-book-ban-push-and-toll-increases-for-out-of-state-visitors)). |
| Health care | Opposed the Affordable Care Act and repeatedly voted for repeal while in the Senate; led passage of the bipartisan Comprehensive Addiction and Recovery Act addressing opioid addiction ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Ayotte); [National Governors Association](https://www.nga.org/governor/kelly-ayotte/)). |
| Abortion | Promised in 2024 to veto legislation further restricting abortion beyond New Hampshire’s current law; vetoed House Bill 232, saying existing federal conscience protections were sufficient ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/politics/2026-07-03/ayotte-vetoes-nine-bills-including-book-ban-push-and-toll-increases-for-out-of-state-visitors)). |
| LGBTQ rights | Vetoed HB 148, which would have allowed some spaces to segregate by biological sex; the evidence also states that she opposed same-sex marriage earlier in her career ([CitizensCount](https://www.citizenscount.org/nh/hillsborough/goffstown); [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Kelly-Ayotte)). |
| Education and school funding | Supports school choice and innovative charter schools but vetoed a bill to study making a charter-school model the default for public schools; vetoed a bill making it easier to remove books and other materials from public schools ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/politics/2026-07-03/ayotte-vetoes-nine-bills-including-book-ban-push-and-toll-increases-for-out-of-state-visitors)). |
| Housing | Said she hoped to increase the housing supply but provided no specifics ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2026-01-09/governor-ayotte-nh-new-hampshire-reelection-first-term-nh-politics)). |
| Immigration | Supported outlawing municipal sanctuary policies and backed an agreement allowing New Hampshire State Police to participate in federal immigration-enforcement actions ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2026-01-09/governor-ayotte-nh-new-hampshire-reelection-first-term-nh-politics)). |
| Environment and energy | Supported the Obama administration’s Clean Power Plan while in the Senate; opposed cap-and-trade and a carbon tax ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Ayotte)). |
| Foreign policy | Took hawkish positions on national security, including opposing the Iran nuclear deal, supporting sanctions on Iran and North Korea, and calling for increased U.S. airstrikes against ISIS ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Ayotte)). |
| EFA vouchers | Her administration expanded New Hampshire’s voucher-like school-choice program ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2026-01-09/governor-ayotte-nh-new-hampshire-reelection-first-term-nh-politics)). |

## Free State / MAGA Ties
- Free State Project: None found in the evidence.
- MAGA: None found in the evidence. The evidence states that she kept former President Donald Trump at a distance during much of her first gubernatorial term ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/incumbent-gop-governor-rolls-primary-victory-keeping-trump-distance)).
- Donald Trump: Ayotte initially supported Trump in 2016, later rescinded that support and said she would write in Mike Pence, and expressed support for Trump as the Republican nominee in 2024 ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Kelly-Ayotte); [Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/01/09/metro/nh-kelly-ayotte-governor-facts/)). Trump’s White House asked her in 2017 to help guide Neil Gorsuch’s confirmation ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/01/09/metro/nh-kelly-ayotte-governor-facts/)).

## Criticism From the Left
- Democratic challenger Cinde Warmington criticized Ayotte for not standing up adequately to President Donald Trump, citing health-care cuts, tariffs, and the war in Iran ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/incumbent-gop-governor-rolls-primary-victory-keeping-trump-distance)).
- Democrats criticized Ayotte’s role supporting Neil Gorsuch’s confirmation, noting that Gorsuch later joined the Supreme Court decision overturning Roe v. Wade ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/01/09/metro/nh-kelly-ayotte-governor-facts/)).
- The left criticized her 2025–26 state budget for cuts to state agencies ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/incumbent-gop-governor-rolls-primary-victory-keeping-trump-distance)).

## Criticism From the Right
- During the 2024 Republican primary, an opponent criticized Ayotte as too moderate on immigration enforcement and other issues ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/01/09/metro/nh-kelly-ayotte-governor-facts)).
- Americans for Prosperity declined to support her 2016 Senate reelection campaign because of her support for the Clean Power Plan; coverage described the group as viewing her as too moderate ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/01/09/metro/nh-kelly-ayotte-governor-facts); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Ayotte)).
- Republican lawmakers backed several bills Ayotte vetoed in 2026, including measures concerning abortion-related conscience rights, tolls, charter schools, and school-content removal ([NHPR](https://www.nhpr.org/politics/2026-07-03/ayotte-vetoes-nine-bills-including-book-ban-push-and-toll-increases-for-out-of-state-visitors)).
